Qasm privacy policy

Effective 2026-09-04. Applies to the Qasm app for iOS and Android.

Qasm is a private scheduler. You record which household you stay at each night, changes to that plan, gifts you buy, and notes. This page says exactly what the app stores, where, who can read it, and how to take it out.

What is collected

  • Your email address. Used to sign you in with a one-time code and to identify your account. Nothing else is sent to it.
  • What you record in the app. Household names, the rotation you set, nights marked away or changed, makeup nights, gift titles, amounts, currencies, dates and categories, and any notes you type. All of it is linked to your account.
  • Settings. Whether the app lock is on, your default currency.

That is the whole list. The app has no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting that captures screen contents, and no third-party tracking.

What is not collected

Location, contacts, photos, health data, device identifiers beyond what the operating system needs to deliver the app, and anything about other people beyond the household names you choose to type.

Where it is stored

On Convex (convex.dev), a cloud database service, in the United States. Data travels between your device and Convex over TLS. At rest it is encrypted at the infrastructure level by Convex. It is not encrypted at the field level: household names, notes, gift titles and amounts are stored as readable text.

Who can read it

  • You, on any device where you sign in.
  • Hikmah Labs, the operator of the app, through the database's administrative tools. This access is used only to run the service and to help with a support request you make.
  • Convex, as the hosting provider, under its own terms.

Nobody else. The app does not sell, share, or license your data.

The app lock

The optional app lock asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode when you return to the app. It is a gate on the device, not encryption. Anyone who can sign in to your account can still read your data.

Notifications

If reminders are enabled in a future version, their text will never name a household on the lock screen.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. There is no automatic retention or backup copy beyond what the database service keeps for operational recovery, which expires on its own schedule.

Export

Settings → Your data → Export everything produces one JSON file containing all of the above. You choose where it goes through the system share sheet. The app keeps no copy of that file.

Deletion

Settings → Your data → Delete account removes every row that belongs to you, including your sign-in, immediately and permanently. It is not a flag on a record; the rows are gone. There is no recovery, so export first if you want a copy.

Children

Qasm is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it and the app will point to the new version. Continued use after a change means you accept it.
